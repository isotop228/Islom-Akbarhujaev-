01 · Daily life

A Day in My Life

School, goals, and a little free time.

8:00 AM — The day begins!
My day usually starts with getting ready for school. Honestly, leaving my bed in the morning is not always easy! After breakfast, I pack my things and head to school.

During the day — Student mode: ON.
At school, I study different subjects, talk to my classmates, and try to do my best. Some lessons are easier than others, but I know that learning takes time and effort. I want to build a good future for myself, so I take my education seriously.

After school — Time to focus.
When I get home, I usually do my homework. I also practise English because I want to speak confidently and understand more films and videos without subtitles. Sometimes I learn new words, and sometimes I make mistakes. That’s normal!

Evening — Finally, free time!
I like watching films or playing games. They help me relax after a busy day. My favourite part is the evening because I can slow down and enjoy my time.

02 · Food & culture

My Comfort Food

Good food, good mood. What makes a meal special?

Let’s talk about something that can make almost any day better: food!

For me, food is more than something we eat when we are hungry. It can bring people together, create memories, and make an ordinary day feel special.

I enjoy different kinds of food, especially when it is freshly prepared and shared with family or friends. I do not think a meal has to be expensive or come from a fancy restaurant to be amazing. Sometimes, a simple homemade meal is exactly what you need after a long day.

One of my favourite things about trying new dishes is discovering different flavours. Every country has its own food traditions, and food is a fun way to learn about other cultures. I would love to try more international dishes in the future.

If I had to choose between eating alone and sharing a delicious meal with people I care about, I would choose the second option. The conversation can make the whole experience better.

03 · Hobbies & happiness

My Happiness Playlist

Films, games, and goals that keep me going.

Everyone has something that helps them feel better after a long day. For me, three things make a big difference: films, games, and learning English.

Films: A different world.
I enjoy watching films because they let me experience different stories and meet interesting characters. A good film can make me laugh, surprise me, or even change the way I think. Sometimes, I remember a scene long after the film ends.

Games: Time to switch off.
When I play games, I can take a break from schoolwork and daily problems. I like the challenge of trying to improve and reach the next level. Of course, it is important to balance gaming with studying and other responsibilities.

English: My future superpower.
Learning English is not always easy, but I enjoy noticing my progress. Every new word gives me another way to express my thoughts. I also want to understand films and communicate with people from different countries.

My biggest lesson is that happiness does not always come from big achievements. It can come from a funny film, a good game, or learning something new.

Question of the day: What would you put on your happiness playlist?
